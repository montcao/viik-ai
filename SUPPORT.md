# Getting support

## Something is broken

[Open a bug report](https://github.com/montcao/viik-ai/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Tell us which chat assistant you used (Claude or ChatGPT), what you asked viik to do, and what happened.

## You have an idea

[Open a feature request](https://github.com/montcao/viik-ai/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Describe the problem you're trying to solve, not only the solution you have in mind.

## You have a question

Ask in [Q&A Discussions](https://github.com/montcao/viik-ai/discussions/categories/q-a). Someone else may have asked already, so search first. For open-ended ideas and feedback, use [Discussions](https://github.com/montcao/viik-ai/discussions).

## Anything private

Email **support@viik.ai** for anything about your own data, privacy requests (access, correction, deletion), or anything you'd rather not post publicly. Say which chat assistant you used and what happened. Please do not send your resume.

## Security problems

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.

## Before you post

GitHub issues are public and indexed by search engines. **Never include your resume, name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details.** We'll remove issues that contain personal data.

## Response times

viik is early software maintained by a small team. We read every issue but can't promise a response time.
