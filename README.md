# viik

**Finding a career should be easy.**

[viik](https://viik.ai) reviews your resume, formats it without changing your wording, finds public job postings that match it, and scores how well it fits a role. It all happens inside the chat assistant you already use, Claude or ChatGPT.

viik never submits an application or contacts an employer for you. You stay in control of every next step.

> **Status:** viik is free, early software that is still being built. The website is a preview: public chat connections are still being verified, so viik is not open to everyone yet.

This repository is viik's public home for bug reports, feature requests and questions. viik's source code is not published here.

## What viik does

- **Review my resume.** See what works and what to improve first. Every finding points to a line in your own resume.
- **Format my resume.** Get a simple layout that keeps your existing wording and facts. Download it as PDF or Word.
- **Find my best-matching jobs.** See public job postings ranked by how much wording they share with your resume, with filters for remote work, location and how recently the role was posted.
- **Fixed checks.** viik checks reading order, text versus images, file size, contact details, dates and standard headings. No AI is involved in these checks.

A score is not your rank among applicants or your chance of getting hired.

**Not available yet:** saved resumes, creating a resume from scratch, and automatic applications.

## Getting started

viik works as a connector in your chat assistant. See [viik.ai](https://viik.ai/#get-started) to add it to Claude or ChatGPT, and read the [docs](https://viik.ai/docs/) for a walkthrough.

## Your data

viik holds a resume in server memory only, never on disk, and deletes it 30 minutes after it was last read, or two hours after you first sent it at the latest. There is no resume database. viik does not use your resume to train AI, show you ads, or share it with employers or anyone else. Your conversation itself is kept by your chat assistant's provider under its own policy.

Full details are in the [privacy policy](https://viik.ai/privacy/).
