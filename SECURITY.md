# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security problems in public GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests.**

Email **support@viik.ai** with:

- A description of the problem and its impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Any relevant URLs, chat assistant (Claude or ChatGPT), and approximate time

Do not include real people's resumes or personal data in your report. Use made-up test data.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated as we investigate, and let you know when it's fixed. Please give us reasonable time to fix the problem before disclosing it publicly.

## Scope

In scope:

- The viik service and its chat connectors
- The viik.ai website

Out of scope:

- The chat assistants themselves (Claude, ChatGPT). Report those to Anthropic or OpenAI.
- Third-party job boards whose public postings viik reads
- Denial-of-service, spam, or social-engineering attacks

## Safe harbor

We won't pursue legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and doesn't access or keep other people's data.
